WarCry Unit Roster

Three factions: Human, Orc, Undead. Every unit and mob belongs to at least one, recolored to that faction's rules. Swatches are sampled from the shared palette textures (the middle tone is shown in hex). Units tagged in web are loaded by the prototype; Unity only ones still need import-assets. Mobs come from the game's mob list: click one to open its animation viewer.
